How it works

Unusual trading, checked by facts, judged by {{ st.models|length }} AIs.

Every weekday evening it looks at every company on NSE and BSE and keeps the few worth a closer look.

  1. 1{{ icon('download', 28, '', 'd') }}

    Collect

    The day's closing price, volume and delivery for ~5,000 companies, straight from NSE and BSE.

  2. 2{{ icon('volume', 28, '', 'd') }}

    Spot the shock

    Keep stocks trading over {{ st.filters.volume_spike_multiple|round(1) }}× their usual volume this week. Big money often shows up as volume first.

  3. 3{{ icon('scale', 28, '', 'd') }}

    Check the business

    Drop anything small, loss-making, expensive or in debt:

    Market cap > ₹{{ st.filters.min_market_cap_cr|int }} CrP/E < {{ st.filters.max_pe|int }}ROE > {{ st.filters.min_roe_pct|int }}%Debt/equity < {{ st.filters.max_debt_to_equity }}

  4. 4{{ icon('council', 28, '', 'd') }}

    Ask the council

    Each survivor gets a fact sheet and a news search. All AIs read the same facts and vote Buy, Hold or Sell with a score out of 10.

{{ icon('shield', 22, '', 'd') }}Facts

Computed from official filings, the same every time: cash flow, debt, promoter pledge, who's buying, delivery, ratios.

{{ icon('council', 22, '', 'd') }}Opinions

Only the judgement comes from AI: outlook, risks, the call. When they split, the page says so.
